# Help — Mustajab ud Dua (Nawabi Nuskha)

> **اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمَاتِ**
>
> *Allahummaghfir lee wa lil mu'mineena wal mu'minaati wal muslimeena wal muslimaat.*
>
> “O Allah, forgive me and the believing men and believing women, and the Muslim men and Muslim women.”

This file mirrors the in-app **Help** tab (`⌘3`).

## What is the nuskha?

Read this dua **25 times — or 27 times — in one sitting**, after your prayers or at any fixed
time of day. The title means “the recipe for having your dua answered”: it is a *mustajab*
(accepted) supplication when read with attention and sincerity.

## What does the dua ask for?

Forgiveness for you, and for every believing man and woman, and every Muslim man and woman —
a broad, generous supplication that extends far beyond yourself.

## Which hadith is it from?

Reported by Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi and others: this dua is mustajab when read 25 — or 27 — times.
It is also taught for someone who has passed away.

## How do I count?

**Counter** tab → press `Space` or `Return`, click the ring, or use the big **Count 1** button.
`Esc` (or the `−` button) undoes the last count; **Reset** clears today.

## 25 or 27 — which is better?

Both are reported. Pick one and stay consistent: **Settings → Dua target → 25 / 27**.

## Does the counter reset?

Yes. Counts are stored per calendar day and roll over automatically at midnight. The streak
counts consecutive days on which you reached the target (today or yesterday, if today is not
finished yet).

## Reminders

One local notification each day at the time you choose (default `07:00`). Toggle it, change
the time, or send a test notification from **Settings → Daily reminder**. Notifications are
scheduled by macOS on your Mac — no server, no account, no network.

## Where is my data?

Local only, in this app’s preferences (`defaults` domain `com.ikbhal.mustajabdua`). Nothing is
uploaded anywhere.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Action | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Count one recitation | `Space` / `Return` |
| Undo last count | `Esc` |
| Counter · History · Help · Settings | `⌘1` · `⌘2` · `⌘3` · `⌘4` |

## Onboarding again

**Settings → Show onboarding on next launch**, then relaunch the app — or **Show onboarding
now**.

## Install / uninstall

Run `scripts/build_app.sh`: it builds the app, copies it to `/Applications`, and adds a Desktop
shortcut. Uninstall by deleting `/Applications/MustajabDua.app` and the Desktop shortcut. See
[docs/INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md).
